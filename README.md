# Learn Digital Health and Interoperability

Hello and welcome to this repository!

Would you like to learn how health systems exchange and use data, and how health data spaces support sharing across organizations and borders? Here you’ll find a curated collection of online courses, video lectures, practical guides, articles, and publications that take you from the fundamentals to real-world implementation.

## Start with the basics

If you’re new to interoperability, begin with Catherine Staes’s three-part introduction from the University of Utah.

1. [Part 1 — Introduction to interoperability](https://mediaspace.utah.edu/playlist/dedicated/1_g4nr8pm0/1_sc4s3lfg)
2. [Part 2 — Terminology and data exchange standards](https://mediaspace.utah.edu/playlist/dedicated/1_g4nr8pm0/1_54bql655)
3. [Part 3 — The role of public health experts](https://mediaspace.utah.edu/playlist/dedicated/1_g4nr8pm0/1_uvt8uyvn)

## Dive deeper

- **Health data standards and terminologies** — See how FHIR structures a lab result for exchange, how LOINC identifies the test, and how SNOMED CT represents clinical findings.
- **Interoperability architecture** — Explore how electronic health records, laboratories, registries, and reporting systems such as DHIS2 connect.
- **Laws and governance** — Learn how laws and policies shape who can access, share, and reuse health data in Africa and beyond.
- **Health data spaces** — Explore the African and European Health Data Spaces and how data can be shared for care and reused for research or policymaking.

Search the collection or filter by topic, resource type, level, cost, and certificate availability to find resources that suit your learning goals.
