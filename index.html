Resources and Materials to Learn Health Data Interoperability and Health Data Spaces

This is a collection of online courses, video lectures, PDFs, blogs, and publications for anyone learning about health data interoperability and health data spaces. It covers how health systems exchange and interpret data, the standards and architectures that make exchange possible, and the laws and policies that govern it. It also brings together resources on the African Health Data Space and the European Health Data Space.

If you’re new to the topic, start with the University of Utah’s three-part introduction. Then use the filters below to find resources by topic, format, level, cost, or certificate availability.

Join our Zulip community

Keep the conversation going after the Addis workshop. Our Zulip community is a place to ask questions about the resources on this page, share what has worked in your country or organization, and discuss practical challenges with health data standards, interoperability, and health data spaces.

To join, use the invitation link shared by the workshop organizers. If you haven’t received it, ask the organizers for an invitation.

  • #Welcome — Introduce yourself in the greetings topic and share one question or use case you’d like to explore.
  • #interoperability-questions — Start a topic for a technical or governance question, or reply to an existing discussion.
  • #learning-resources — Share a course, article, or guide and tell others what you found helpful.
